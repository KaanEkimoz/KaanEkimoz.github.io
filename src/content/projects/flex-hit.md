A VR casual game where everything came down to physics feel. The brief was simple — make it accessible, quick to pick up, satisfying to land hits — but the underlying Unity Physics tuning was the whole job.

Working daily with an experienced designer reframed how I think about VR. The hardware imposes constraints you can't ignore: physical fatigue, presence, comfort. Every interaction was tested in headset, every iteration measured in "how long can a new player play before their arms get tired."

## What VR taught me

- **Physics ≠ realism.** VR feel is tuned, not simulated.
- **Accessibility is design, not a checkbox.** Seated mode, height calibration, comfort options.
- **Test in headset constantly.** Editor-only iteration lies to you.
