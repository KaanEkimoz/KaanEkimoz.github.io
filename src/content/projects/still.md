A 3-day game jam build. Team of two developers and one artist, racing against the clock to ship something coherent.

Game jams force a kind of architectural honesty: there's no time to over-engineer, but there's also no time to make a mess. We landed on a minimal puzzle framework — each puzzle is a scene, each scene shares a tiny core of input/animation/state, and the whole thing fits in a few hundred lines.

## What jams teach

- **Scope is the only knob that matters.** Pick something boring and finish it.
- **Polish 5 things, not 50.** A jam build with three solid puzzles beats one with twelve broken ones.
- **Sleep is part of the schedule.** You can't ship a fun game tired.
