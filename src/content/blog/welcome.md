Hey, I'm Kaan. This is the first post on my new portfolio + blog.

## Why a new site?

For a long time my web presence was a static HTML page from years ago — fine, but it didn't feel like *me*. I'm a game developer who cares about feel, performance, and the small details that make experiences memorable. The site should reflect that. So I rebuilt it.

## What's here

- **Projects** — six-plus years of mobile, PC, and VR work, with detail pages for each game.
- **Blog** — devlogs, post-mortems, and occasional thoughts on the craft.

## How it was built

This site was built collaboratively with [Claude](https://claude.com), Anthropic's AI assistant. The stack: **Astro 5** for content + static generation, **Tailwind CSS** for styling, **TypeScript** strict mode, deployed on **GitHub Pages**.

The workflow was conversational. I described the aesthetic I wanted — *modern game-dev portfolio with arcade-UI dashes of color* — and Claude scaffolded the design system, components, and content schemas. We iterated on a few decisions (cool palette over warm, CSS parallax over Three.js, signed-off feature flags rather than premature 3D) before settling on the version you're looking at.

The whole thing — from blank repo to live deployment — went up in a single evening. I called the shots; Claude wrote the code; the result is something I can iterate on for years without fighting the framework.

## A bit about me

I've been part of the software and game development world since 2020, after starting with a degree in Digital Game Design. What began as curiosity about programming and game engines became my primary focus, and over the years I've worked on projects spanning mobile, PC, and VR.

I specialize in mobile game development — there's something uniquely satisfying about building tight, polished experiences that fit in your pocket. From hyper-casual prototypes built in two weeks to roguelike systems developed over five months, I enjoy the full spectrum of gameplay programming, system design, and the small details that make a game feel alive.
