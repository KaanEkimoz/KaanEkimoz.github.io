Privacy Policy — Draw Rush

Developer: Ekimoz Games (Kaan Usta) · Contact: kaanekimoz@gmail.com
Effective date: November 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Draw Rush ("the Game", package / bundle ID com.ekimozgames.drawrush, available on Android through Google Play and on iOS through the App Store) handles information. By playing the Game you agree to this policy. Please also read the Terms of Service.

1. Information the Game collects directly

Draw Rush does not have accounts or sign-in and does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location. Your game progress and settings (level, coins, skins, upgrades, language and sound settings) are stored only on your device and are never sent to us.

2. Advertising and third-party services

The Game shows banner, interstitial and rewarded ads (for example a second chance, double coins, or starting with a shield) through Unity LevelPlay, which includes Unity Ads and the ad networks that LevelPlay mediates. To serve and measure ads, these ad networks may automatically collect and process certain data, including:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (the Google Advertising ID on Android, the IDFA on iOS)
  • Device information (model, operating system, language, coarse region)
  • Ad interactions needed to deliver and measure ads

This data is handled under Unity's privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy. The Game does not use Google AdMob.

3. Personalized ads consent

In regions covered by the GDPR, the Game asks on first launch whether you agree to personalized ads. You can change this answer at any time with the "Personalized ads" switch on the Game's Settings screen. If you say no, ads are still shown, but they are not personalized.

4. Analytics / crash reporting

The Game does not include any separate analytics or crash-reporting SDK. We only see the install and crash reports that Google Play Console and App Store Connect provide to developers.

5. Children

The Game is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Data sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information. Data processed for advertising is shared only with the advertising partners named above, solely to display and measure ads.

7. Your choices

  • Turn personalized ads on or off with the "Personalized ads" switch in the Game's Settings.
  • On iOS, control app tracking in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • On Android, reset or delete your advertising ID, or limit ad personalization, in Settings → Google → Ads.
  • The Game's "Reset game" option deletes all progress stored on your device.
  • Uninstalling the Game removes all of its locally stored data from your device.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Effective date" above shows the latest revision.
